Your Volvo,
on your wrist.

VolvoWatch puts your car's live status in the glance carousel and gives you a status page, a charging page, a trip page and a warnings page — plus climate control — without reaching for your phone.

01

What it shows

Page 1

Status

Combined petrol and electric range as a single number, lock state, whether every door and window is shut, and a service reminder when due.

Page 2

Charging

Battery percentage, AC/DC, and time to full while plugged in — the battery figure turns green the moment it's actively charging.

Page 3

Trip

A large odometer — handy when a fuel card asks for the mileage — average fuel consumption, and both trip meters.

Page 4

Warnings

A real list, not just an icon: doors and windows called out separately, service due, washer fluid, tyre pressure — or a plain "no active warnings".

Command

Climate control

Start and stop climatisation from the watch, with a confirmation step. The app reports what the car actually answered, not just that a command was sent.

Carousel

Glance card

Battery, fuel and range at a glance from the widget carousel, before you open anything.

Setup

  1. 1 Install the app. First launch asks you to connect.
  2. 2 Open the action menu (press select) → "Open connect page" — sends a notification to your phone. Or just open volvowatchapp.tallimedia.com/link directly in any browser.
  3. 3 Sign in with your Volvo ID. You sign in on Volvo's own page — this app never sees your password.
  4. 4 You'll get a short pairing code. Enter it in Garmin Connect Mobile's app settings, or directly on the watch with the on-screen code wheels.
  5. 5 The watch exchanges the code once for a private device token and starts showing your car.

Requirements & limits

CarA Volvo with connected services (Volvo On Call, ~2010–2024, or a Google built-in car from 2020 on) and a Volvo ID with the car linked.
ConnectionYour phone nearby, or the watch on WiFi — no LTE on this device, so it needs one of those to reach the backend.
Devicesfēnix 6/7/8/9, epix 2, venu 2/3, vívoactive 5/6, forerunner 165–970, Marq, Descent — 115 device SKUs.
Not yetLevel 2 actions (remote lock, unlock, flash the lights, honk) need a separate, more restricted approval from Volvo and aren't implemented yet.
FreshnessReadings are as fresh as the car's last check-in — a parked car can go hours between updates, so the app always shows how old the data is.
PrivacyNo analytics, no third-party trackers.
